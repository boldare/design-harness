The foundations read what a source's components use outside its tokens (paddings and radii typed by hand, colours off the palette, text with no style) and settle each value in the proposal: mapped to a token, a proposed token, or a decision. The source is never changed, and the new section of `docs/foundations.md` maps the old values to the tokens. The Figma adapter has the read-only tally for a Figma source.
