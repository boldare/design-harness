The foundations proposal splits its open points into decisions (numbered, with options and a recommended one) and defaults (a value approved with the proposal unless the designer changes it), and its reply shows every open point instead of a selection. In Claude Code the decisions are asked with the question picker, the approval as the last question; without it they are listed so the designer can answer "1a, 2b". Figma and Claude Design are asked about after approval, not as open points.
