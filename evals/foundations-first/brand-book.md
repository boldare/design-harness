# Kestrel brand book

Kestrel plans cycling routes: quiet roads, gravel and bike paths, with the climbs and the cafés on the way. This book is for the people who design and build the Kestrel app. It is version 1.2, from March 2026. Kestrel is a made-up brand, used to test the design-harness workflow.

## Personality

Calm, outdoorsy and exact. Kestrel is a riding partner who knows the roads, not a coach shouting splits. The interface stays out of the way of the map: light surfaces, one strong colour, and plain words.

## Colour

Kestrel has one look: light. The map is always light, so the app is too.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Teal | #0F5C63 | The brand colour. Primary buttons, links, the selected route, the logo. |
| Deep Ink | #14213D | Headings and body text. |
| Slate | #5B6475 | Secondary text: distances, timestamps, hints under fields. |
| Mist | #EEF2F3 | The page background behind cards and lists. |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards, sheets, inputs, and text on Kestrel Teal. |
| Line | #D5DCDF | Borders of inputs and cards, dividers between list rows. |
| Ember | #E4572E | Warnings: a road closure, a route that leaves the paved network. Use it sparingly. |
| Sun | #F6C445 | Highlights: a "new" badge, a personal best. A fill only, never text on white. |

Teal is the only colour that acts. Never use Ember for a button.

## Typography

One typeface: **Work Sans** (Google Fonts), in Regular 400, Medium 500 and SemiBold 600.

| Style | Size / line height | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 32 / 40 | 600 | The screen title, one per screen. |
| Heading | 22 / 28 | 600 | Section titles. |
| Body | 16 / 24 | 400 | Running text, list rows, field values. |
| Body small | 14 / 20 | 400 | Secondary lines and hints. |
| Label | 14 / 20 | 500 | Field labels and button text. |
| Caption | 12 / 16 | 500 | Map chips and metadata, in capitals with 4 % letter spacing. |

## Spacing

Everything sits on a 4 px grid. The steps we use are 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 48. Screens have 16 px side margins; cards have 16 px padding; list rows are 12 px apart.

## Shape

- Corners: 8 px on buttons and inputs, 16 px on cards and sheets, fully round on chips and avatars.
- Borders are 1 px, in Line.
- Cards on Mist lift with one soft shadow: 0 2px 8px, Deep Ink at 8 %. Nothing else has a shadow.

## Controls

Buttons and inputs are 48 px high. A primary button is Kestrel Teal with a white label; a secondary button is white with a 1 px Line border and a Teal label.

## Voice

Short and concrete. Say what is on the road: "Gravel for 3 km after the bridge", not "Surface conditions may vary". Never "Oops".
